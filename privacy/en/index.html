Privacy policy of Τετράδιο

Version of 26 October 2026. Covers the @TetradioBot Mini App in Telegram, the bot and the tetradio.app website.

Who is responsible

The author of Τετράδιο. Contact: “Написать автору” (Write to the author) in the app’s “Настройки и данные” (Settings and data) or a message to @TetradioBot. There is no contact channel outside Telegram.

What is stored on your device

  • The app database: course lessons, words and phrases, your answers, written answers, the review schedule, settings. The database name contains your Telegram id.
  • Telegram’s browser storage: haptics and hint settings, update bookkeeping, scroll positions, a draft of your message to the author; for the duration of a launch — Telegram launch data (id, first and last name, username, language, link parameter).
  • A queue of error reports not yet sent.
  • The microphone is used only in “Речь” (Speaking) exercises: the recording stays in device memory and is not sent anywhere.
  • A full backup is a file you save and share yourself.

Telegram cloud

To carry your progress across devices, the app saves a snapshot to Telegram’s cloud storage (CloudStorage): completed and installed lessons, task results, card states and summaries, statistics (daily — for 14 days), course pauses, the device identifier and platform label. Answer history and written answers are not included. Telegram runs this storage under its own policy.

Messages to the author

You do not enter your name, username or language: Telegram provides them. A message to the bot in its chat (text, photos, files, voice messages) is copied into a separate topic of a private support group in Telegram; the topic title contains your name and @username, its first message — name, @username, id and language. The “Написать автору” form in the app sends Telegram’s signed launch data (it contains id, name, username, language, profile photo, Premium and write-permission flags, launch time and identifiers), your text, the app version, the platform, an error report identifier (from the crash screen) and whether the bot may message you. Id, first and last name, username and language are used; the rest is not stored. If the bot cannot message you yet, Telegram asks for permission before sending — without it the author cannot reply.

Messages are processed by a program on Cloudflare Workers at the nearest Cloudflare network location; when the form is sent, Cloudflare sees the device’s IP address and browser. The program’s logs are off. A Cloudflare D1 database (Western Europe region) stores the user and topic ids, the time of the last confirmation and the last form, a block flag, the error report identifier from a crash-screen link and when it was received (used for one day, cleared by your next message to the bot). The program does not store message texts: they remain in your chat with the bot and in the Telegram group. An error report identifier sent from the crash screen links that report to you in the topic.

Error reports

On a failure the app sends a report to Sentry (USA): error type and message, stack, the screen path and up to 30 recent navigations without parameters (paths may contain lesson or word identifiers), requests to course files (path and status), Mini App and sync service events (minimising, window sizes, sync duration and size), browser and system details Sentry derives from the request. Tags: env, app.version, app.build, tg.version, tg.platform, report.category. Extra fields: category, kind, packageId, packageVersion, componentStack. Reports contain no words, answers, progress, launch data or Telegram name. Sentry receives the connection’s IP address; whether it stores it depends on the project settings.

Reports are on by default and are switched off in “Настройки и данные” with “Отправлять отчёты об ошибках” (Send error reports); the queue is deleted at the same time. If the app database fails to open, the setting cannot be read: reports of that launch are sent regardless of the switch.

Website and other requests

Pages, lessons and audio are served by GitHub Pages; GitHub sees the IP address of requests. The app loads the Telegram script from telegram.org. Google Cloud Text-to-Speech audio is prepared in advance: the app makes no requests to Google. Everything else is voiced by the device’s speech synthesis; it may use the system vendor’s network voices and send them the text of course phrases, without your data.

What the app does not use

No analytics, ads, cookies or registration. The app does not ask for your phone number, contacts, location or payments. There are no automated decisions with legal effects: the review schedule is a learning feature.

Purposes and legal bases

  • Learning and carrying progress across devices — performing what you requested by opening the course (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Error reports — legitimate interest in finding and fixing failures; reports contain no learning content or Telegram data and can be switched off (Art. 6(1)(f)). Reports of a launch in which the database failed to open — the same interest, without the switch.
  • Messages to the author — replying to your request (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f)).

Providing data is not mandatory: the app does not work without Telegram; it works without reports and messages.

Who receives data

Processors: Sentry (reports), Cloudflare (messages to the author), GitHub (website). Telegram is an independent controller of the cloud storage and the support group under its own policy. Sentry, Cloudflare and GitHub process data in the USA as well — under the mechanisms these companies use (the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the EU standard contractual clauses): Sentry, Cloudflare, GitHub.

Retention

  • Device and Telegram cloud — until you delete them with “Удалить мои данные” (Delete my data) in “Настройки и данные”; repeat it on every device that has progress.
  • The report queue — until sent, until reports are switched off or until your data is deleted.
  • The group conversation and the D1 record — until you tick “И переписку с автором” (And my conversation with the author) when deleting your data, ask for deletion via “Написать автору”, or the author deletes them. If the author blocked you in the conversation, only your id and the block flag remain after deletion — legitimate interest in preventing abuse (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Reports in Sentry — for the project’s retention period in Sentry.

Your rights

You can access, rectify and erase your data, restrict and object to processing (for reports — the switch in “Настройки и данные”) and receive your data in a portable form (“Сохранить полную копию”, Save a full backup).

  • Data on the device and in the Telegram cloud is deleted by the “Удалить мои данные” button in “Настройки и данные”. Messages in your chat with the bot are deleted by you.
  • The group conversation and the D1 record are deleted by the same button with “И переписку с автором” ticked, or by the author on request via “Написать автору”.
  • The button does not delete Sentry reports: a specific report can be found and deleted by its identifier from “Скопировать диагностику” (Copy diagnostics) on the crash screen — without it the report cannot be linked to you.

You can lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority of your EU country or of Cyprus — the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection (Επίτροπος Προστασίας Δεδομένων Προσωπικού Χαρακτήρα), dataprotection.gov.cy.

Age

The app is not intended for persons under 16. Age is not verified.
